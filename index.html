Website Plan

How We Make BHD Asia Show Up on Google

The current website exists but is hard to find on Google. These steps will fix that — organized into three approaches that work together.

SEO

Be Found on Google

When someone types "TRE Singapore" or "stress relief workshop Singapore" into Google, we want bhdasia.com to appear on the first page.

1

Fix Page Titles & Descriptions

Every page on the website needs a clear title that includes words people actually search for. Right now most pages don't have this.

2

Add Keywords to the Pages

Words like "TRE Singapore", "nervous system healing Singapore", "somatic coach Singapore" need to appear naturally throughout the website text.

3

Create a Blog / Resources Section

Google rewards websites that regularly publish useful content. We'll add a simple blog with articles about TRE, stress, and the nervous system — 2 articles per month.

4

Fix the Website Speed

Slow websites rank lower on Google. We'll compress images and clean up the code to make the site load faster.

5

Add FAQ Sections to Key Pages

Google loves FAQ content. Adding "What is TRE?" and "Is TRE safe?" type questions on each page boosts ranking.

6

Get Other Websites to Link to BHD Asia

When reputable websites mention bhdasia.com, Google sees it as a trust signal. We'll reach out to wellness directories and partner sites.

AEO

Show Up When People Ask AI

When people ask ChatGPT or Google's AI "What is TRE in Singapore?" we want Isabelle's name and BHD Asia to come up as the answer.

AEO = Answer Engine Optimization — making sure AI tools recommend Isabelle.

1

Add Structured Data (Schema Markup)

This is invisible code that tells Google exactly what BHD Asia is, what events are happening, and who Isabelle is. Simple to add, big impact on how AI reads the site.

2

Write Clear 'About' and 'Expert' Content

AI tools pull from authoritative pages. We write a clear page that explains exactly who Isabelle is, her credentials, and what TRE is — in a format AI can easily read.

3

Add FAQ Schema to Every Page

Mark up the FAQ sections so AI tools can quote them directly as answers — increasing the chance Isabelle appears as the expert source.

4

Create a Google Business Profile

Isabelle's practice should appear on Google Maps and in "People Also Ask" boxes when people search locally. This is free and essential.

Why AEO matters now

More people are using ChatGPT and Google AI to find services. If Isabelle isn't mentioned by AI, she's invisible to that audience — and it's growing fast.

GEO

Show Up for Singapore Searches

When someone in Singapore searches for wellness coaching or TRE training, we want BHD Asia to appear at the top — not someone overseas.

GEO = Local SEO — targeting people in Singapore specifically.

1

Set Up / Optimize Google Business Profile

Claim the listing, add photos, add services, add opening hours. This is free and directly improves how BHD Asia appears in local searches.

2

Use Singapore-Specific Keywords

Add "Singapore" to key phrases throughout the site: "TRE trainer Singapore", "somatic coach Singapore", "stress release workshop Singapore".

3

Get Listed in Singapore Directories

Submit BHD Asia to Singapore wellness, coaching, and HR directories. These links tell Google that Isabelle is a local expert.

4

Add Location Pages

A dedicated page about working with clients in Singapore, mentioning specific areas like CBD, Orchard, etc. — shows Google this is a real local business.

5

Encourage Google Reviews

Ask past clients to leave a Google review. Reviews directly improve local search ranking — and they build trust for new visitors.

Organic vs Paid — What's the Difference?

Organic = the regular posts we make every week. Free — but only reaches people who already follow or discover the account naturally.
